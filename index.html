It started in the car.

We were on a long drive, playing the kind of word game you play to make the miles go faster. My wife has a talent for finding the one word in any puzzle that makes everyone in the car groan. She found several, and then she said the thing that turned into this app:

“The dirty words should be worth more.”🌶️😈

She was right. Every word game politely pretends half the vocabulary doesn’t exist — you can spot a filthy little four-letter word sitting right there in the grid, perfectly legal, and score the same four points you’d get for OATS.

Word Vice does the opposite. Ordinary words score the ordinary way; the naughty ones pay a bonus. I built it for her first. The rest of you are welcome to it.

How It Plays

⏱️

Sixty seconds

One round is one minute. Long enough to get good, short enough to say “one more” and mean it.

👆

Swipe to spell

Drag across neighboring letters. The board reads your whole gesture, so fast and sloppy still lands the word you meant. Tapping works too.

🌶️

Vice pays better

Every board hides at least one word you’d be embarrassed to say out loud. Finding it is worth real points.

🤬

Set your own limit

PG-13 keeps it cheeky. R-Rated takes the gloves off. Each level keeps its own boards, scores, and callsigns.

📐

4×4, 5×5, or 6×6 on iPad

Quick and punchy, or a roomier grid with more to dig through.

🔒

Yours alone

No ads, no accounts, no tracking, no internet required. Your scores never leave your phone.

Word Vice is built to work with VoiceOver. If something reads wrong or won’t focus, tell me and I’ll fix it.
