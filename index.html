It started in the car.

We were on a long drive, playing the kind of word game you play to make the miles go faster. My wife has a talent for finding the one word in any puzzle that makes everyone in the car blush. She found several, and then she said the thing that turned into this app:

“The dirty words should be worth more.”🌶️😈

She was right. Every word game politely pretends half the vocabulary doesn’t exist. You can spot a filthy little four-letter word sitting right there in the grid, perfectly legal, and score the same four points you’d get for OATS.

Word Vice does the opposite. Ordinary words score the ordinary way; the naughty ones pay a bonus. I built it for her first. The rest of you are welcome to it.

How to Misbehave

📅

One board a day

A fresh 4×4 at midnight, the same for everyone, no clock. Find one vice word to win the day, or find them all to show off. Fridays come loaded.

🔥

Keep the streak alive

Win every day and watch the number climb. Night owls get until 2 a.m., because some of our best sinning happens after midnight.

🫙

Fill the jar

Every vice you find goes in your jar of bad words, for good. iCloud carries it, and your streak, to your other devices.

⏱️

Or race the clock

Need more? Sixty-second rounds, as many as you like, on 4×4, 5×5, or 6×6 on iPad. Find a vice for Vice Fever, where its letters pay extra.

🤬

Choose your corruption level

PG-13 keeps it cheeky. R-Rated takes the gloves off. At PG-13 the R words still turn up, censored, like a dare.
